## Implementation

- `package_reader::ogg` (new): page parsing and packet reassembly with granules.
- `package_reader::vorbis` (new), a Vorbis I decoder:
  - codebooks (sparse/ordered lengths, canonical codewords, lookup types 1/2);
  - floor 1 (decode and curve synthesis with the inverse-dB table);
  - residues 0/1/2;
  - mapping 0 with square-polar inverse coupling;
  - the Vorbis power-complementary windows for both block sizes;
  - an FFT-based inverse MDCT checked against its definition;
  - overlap-add from block centre to block centre.

  Floor 0 is rejected; no installed stream uses it.
- `scd` reads codec 6:
  - the aux-chunk offset and the miniheader;
  - header pages XOR'ed with the key when version is 2 and the bytes are not `OggS` (FUN_1400ce8a0);
  - PCM `clamp(trunc(f·32767))`;
  - loop start = granule of the page at the loop byte offset + the miniheader skip count; loop end = last granule − 1;
  - the stream trimmed to the last granule.
- `audio_out`:
  - linear fades that hold, stop or pause, and resume;
  - quad streams folded to stereo (front + rear per side) [inferred].
- `title`:
  - the stream decodes on a background thread during the opening black, and the title waits for it;
  - FUN_140537420 starts it one frame after the main layout, at volume 1.0 × the SCD volume;
  - FadeBgm(720) fades it over 720 ms [inferred units] and stops it.
- `test_room` (`music` config entry):
  - room entry starts field track 118 at 0x3000/16383 × the SCD volume (0.5), with no fade;
  - the battle state follows FUN_1403ab6f0: an enemy that is not leaving or dead within 1200 horizontal units, with a 45/120-frame hysteresis (immediate in the first 30 frames);
  - on a switch, FUN_140155c30: to battle, 118 pauses over a 2 s fade and 119 restarts from the top with a 1 s fade-in; to field, 119 fades out over 3 s and stops, and 118 resumes from where it paused over 2 s.
